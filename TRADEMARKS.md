# Trademark policy

The software, documentation, and specification licenses do not grant rights in
the Nova name, logos, certification marks, or other source identifiers.

Until a formal conformance and trademark program exists:

- accurate descriptive statements such as “implements the Nova protocol” or
  “based on Nova” are permitted to the extent allowed by law;
- modified or incompatible projects must not imply endorsement, certification,
  or official status;
- the project logo must not be used to brand a fork or unrelated product
  without written permission; and
- “Nova conforming” or similar certification claims must wait for an approved
  conformance policy.

This file does not assert that any mark is registered or available in every
jurisdiction. Trademark clearance should occur before a public product launch.
