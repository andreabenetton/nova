# Third-party notices

## Historical research papers

The PDFs in `research/original-papers/` contain historical Nova text together
with quotations, figures, tables, or other material originating from third
parties. Bibliographic attribution does not by itself authorize relicensing.
Those PDFs are therefore marked
`LicenseRef-Nova-Research-Draft-Restricted` and are excluded from the repository's
open documentation license pending a rights review.

Before republication, each external figure, table, substantial quotation, and
adapted illustration must be recorded with:

- source and author;
- rights holder where known;
- license or written permission;
- permitted forms of redistribution and adaptation; and
- replacement status if an original Nova diagram will be created.

## Legal texts

The license texts in `LICENSES/` and the Developer Certificate of Origin text
are reproduced as standard third-party legal instruments. They are not authored
by the Nova project and must not be modified.

## Dependencies and services

This repository does not vendor third-party Rust dependencies at this stage.
External GitHub Actions, toolchains, and future dependencies retain their own
licenses. Dependency lock files and software-bill-of-materials generation will
be added when dependency selection begins.
