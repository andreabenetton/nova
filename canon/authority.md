# Design authority

Normative authority descends in this order:

1. Canon
2. Versioned contracts
3. Versioned peer-protocol and wire specifications
4. Versioned integration, simulation-model, and compatibility profiles
5. Accepted ADRs
6. Implementation documentation

Proposed ADRs describe candidate decisions and do not override current normative documents. Research material explains design lineage but does not define conformance.

Simulation implementations and recorded traces are evidence. They do not override the contracts, protocol specifications, or simulation-model documents from which they are derived.
