# Normative language

The key words **MUST**, **MUST NOT**, **REQUIRED**, **SHALL**, **SHALL NOT**,
**SHOULD**, **SHOULD NOT**, **RECOMMENDED**, **MAY**, and **OPTIONAL** in Nova
normative documents are to be interpreted as described in
[RFC 2119](https://www.ietf.org/rfc/rfc2119.txt).

These key words carry their RFC 2119 meanings only when written in uppercase.
Lowercase uses of words such as "must", "should", and "may" are ordinary prose
unless a document explicitly states otherwise.

This rule applies repository-wide to documents identified as normative. A
normative document MAY repeat the notice for self-contained publication, but it
MUST NOT redefine the meanings of these key words.

Machine-readable contracts express requirements through their schema and
fields. When their descriptive text uses the uppercase key words listed above,
this policy also applies.
