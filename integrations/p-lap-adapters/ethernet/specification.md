# Ethernet Adapter specification stub

Define:

- supported Ethernet media and frame forms;
- local and peer locator encoding;
- EtherType or equivalent demultiplexing choice;
- discovery multicast or broadcast address;
- MTU and VLAN handling;
- permissions and raw-frame access;
- receive filtering;
- failure and link-state reporting;
- conformance behavior.

P-LAP authentication, fragmentation semantics, and peer lifecycle remain in the P-LAP protocol.
