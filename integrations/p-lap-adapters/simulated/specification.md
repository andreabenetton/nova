
# Simulated Adapter specification stub

The Simulated Adapter translates P-LAP Adapter operations into Virtual Fabric endpoint and link operations.

Required future detail:

- locator representation;
- broadcast and multicast domains;
- frame boundary preservation;
- MTU and oversize behavior;
- deterministic discovery delivery;
- loss, duplication, reordering, and partition mapping;
- activation and deactivation ordering;
- limits and conformance vectors.
