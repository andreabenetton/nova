# Simulated Binding specification stub

The Simulated Binding translates Binding instances and P-RAP units into Virtual Fabric endpoints and links.

Required future detail:

- simulated Remote locator format;
- reliable and unreliable profiles;
- ordered and unordered profiles;
- effective maximum unit size;
- Binding instance failure and replacement;
- locator rebinding and migration events;
- deterministic congestion and backpressure signaling;
- conformance vectors.
